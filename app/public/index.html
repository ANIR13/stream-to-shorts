▶ Stream → Shorts

Turn a long stream into Shorts

  1. Pick a video: drop it into the source folder or use Import video.
  2. Analyze: the app scans the audio and on-screen action and suggests the strongest moments.
  3. Adjust: trim each clip, set titles, place the facecam and gameplay, choose the caption style.
  4. Render: get vertical 9:16 MP4s with animated captions in the output folder.

Each video is its own project: its clips, captions, word fixes and exports never mix with another video's. Switch between videos at any time; each one keeps its progress and your place.

Tip: for big recordings, copy the file straight into the source folder (instant), then click ⟳. You can also drop a video anywhere on this window.
